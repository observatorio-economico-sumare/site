About the Sumaré Economic Observatory

The Sumaré Economic Observatory (OES) is an independent, nonpartisan, free initiative dedicated to improving access to economic and social data on Sumaré, São Paulo, Brazil.

The project compiles indicators from official sources and is committed to transparent methods, reliable data and accessible presentation. Published information is accompanied by sources, reference periods and relevant limitations.

Its purpose is to broaden access to knowledge and support evidence-based public discussion.

The OES is not part of a government agency or research institution and does not represent the organizations that produce the statistics it uses.

Institutional identity

Mission

Make reliable, verifiable economic and social information about Sumaré freely available in a clear and accessible format.

Vision

Become an independent reference for economic and social information about Sumaré, supporting a better-informed society and evidence-based public discussion.

Values

  • Scientific rigor: commitment to sound methods and verifiable sources.
  • Independence: technical, editorial and political autonomy.
  • Transparency: disclosure of sources, methods and corrections.
  • Accessibility: free, understandable and publicly accessible information.
  • Public interest: commitment to public knowledge and social development.

Statement of independence

The Sumaré Economic Observatory (OES) is an independent, nonpartisan, not-for-profit initiative dedicated to sharing economic and social information in the public interest.

The OES is not part of, and does not represent, government bodies, political parties or research institutions. Use of data produced by these organizations does not imply affiliation or endorsement.

Its work is guided by four commitments:

  1. Editorial independence: decisions about content, methods and publication are based on technical criteria.
  2. Impartiality: data are presented without manipulation to favor political, economic or private interests.
  3. Transparency: partnerships, financial support and any conflicts of interest will be publicly disclosed.
  4. Independence in partnerships: no partnership may dictate results, restrict publications or interfere with technical conclusions.

The OES distinguishes statistical data from interpretation and analysis. Personal opinions and positions do not represent the institution's stance.

About the founder

Aline Araujo Souza is an economist who graduated from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ), with experience in financial and economic analysis, market research, valuation and investments.

She founded the Sumaré Economic Observatory (OES), an independent initiative created to broaden access to local economic and social data and contribute to evidence-based public discussion.

The OES is an independent project and does not represent institutions where its founder has studied or worked.

Editorial policy

The Sumaré Economic Observatory (OES) publishes economic and social information of public interest, prioritizing official sources and documented methods.

Publication standards

  • Identified source: each indicator should identify the producing institution and, where possible, link to the original release.
  • Statistical context: data should include the reference period, geographic coverage, unit of measurement and relevant limitations.
  • Faithful presentation: headings, scales, selections and comparisons must not mislead. Missing data must not be presented as zero.
  • Editorial separation: verifiable facts, OES calculations and interpretative analyses will be clearly distinguished. Correlation will not be presented as causation without appropriate evidence.
  • Validation: automated tools, including AI, may support the work but do not replace source verification or editorial responsibility.

The initial phase prioritizes indicators and methodological notes. Independent analyses, if introduced, will be clearly identified and accompanied by references, assumptions and limitations.

The OES does not produce official statistics or represent its source institutions. Errors are handled under the Audit policy.

Audit and corrections policy

The OES audit process initially consists of internal technical verification, not an independent third-party audit.

Pre-publication verification

Each indicator undergoes checks proportionate to its complexity, including:

  1. Identification and consultation of the original source.
  2. Verification of location, reference period, unit of measurement and indicator definition.
  3. Verification of transcriptions, calculations, aggregations and transformations performed by the OES.
  4. Documentation of the source, access date, procedure and person responsible for verification.

Where the OES performs calculations, the rules and, where feasible, scripts should allow results to be reproduced. Missing, estimated, provisional and revised data will be unambiguously labeled.

Revisions and corrections

Series will be updated according to source availability and frequency. Each indicator will display its reference and OES update dates.

Material errors will be corrected once identified and confirmed. Corrections must state what changed, when it changed and why, with a record in version history. Where an item's reliability is subject to significant doubt, it will be flagged or temporarily removed.

Changes in methodology and revisions made by source institutions will also be documented when they affect comparability.

Transparency policy

The Sumaré Economic Observatory (OES) is committed to making its published data and operating arrangements understandable and verifiable.

Data disclosure

As applicable, each indicator discloses the original source, link to the release, geographic coverage, reference period, measurement unit, access or update date, methodology and relevant limitations.

Responsibility and governance

Aline Araujo Souza founded and is responsible for the project. The OES is independent and does not represent public bodies, political parties, universities or the founder's former employers.

Partnerships, sponsorships, donations, financial support and potential conflicts of interest affecting perceived independence will be publicly disclosed, including the nature of the relationship. Supporters have no authority to impose conclusions or interfere with published results.

Version history and accountability

The project will document sources, calculations and changes in version control. The repository may remain private during development. At public launch, eligible records and materials will be made available without exposing credentials, personal data or protected information.

Correction requests will be evaluated on the evidence and handled under the Audit policy.
