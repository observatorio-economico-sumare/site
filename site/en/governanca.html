About the OES

The Sumaré Economic Observatory is an independent, nonpartisan, not-for-profit initiative offering free public access to local economic and social data.

Mission and values

Mission: make reliable information about Sumaré easier to access.

Vision: become an independent reference for local economic and social information.

Values: rigor, independence, transparency, accessibility and public interest.

Independence

The OES is not part of, and does not represent, municipal government, political parties, universities or data-producing institutions. Methods and publications follow technical criteria without interference from supporters.

Founder

Aline Araujo Souza is an economist who graduated from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ), with experience in financial analysis, market research, valuation and investments. The project does not represent institutions where she studied or worked.

Editorial policy

Each indicator shows its source, reference year and the information needed to understand it. Unverified indicators are not presented as confirmed. OES analysis will be published separately from official figures, with its own sources and explanations.

Auditing and corrections

The OES checks its own data; this is not an independent audit. We verify sources and calculations. When we find an error, we correct it and record what changed, when and why. Figures with significant uncertainty are flagged or removed.

Transparency

Methods, updates and corrections are documented. Relevant partnerships, financial support and conflicts of interest will be disclosed. No supporter may dictate results or prevent publication.
