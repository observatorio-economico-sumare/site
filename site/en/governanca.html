How the OES works

See how we select data, check sources and correct errors.

Independence

The OES is not part of, and does not represent, municipal government, political parties, universities or data-producing institutions. Methods and publications follow technical criteria without interference from supporters.

Editorial policy

Each indicator shows its source, reference year and the information needed to understand it. Unverified indicators are not presented. OES indicators use data from official sources. Independent analyses will be identified separately and based on verifiable data and research.

Checks and corrections

The OES checks its own data; this is not an independent audit. We verify sources and calculations. When we find an error, we correct it and record what changed, when and why. Figures with significant uncertainty are flagged or removed.

Transparency

We disclose data sources, updates and any corrections. We will also disclose partnerships and financial support, preserving OES independence.

Privacy and data protection

The Sumaré Economic Observatory (OES) is an independent initiative under the responsibility of its founder, Aline Araujo Souza.

No registration is required to use the website. Personal data provided through the “Contact us” form are used solely to receive and reply to messages, review suggestions and check potential corrections, based on legitimate interests under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

The website uses GitHub and Google services, which may receive technical access information.

Information is stored in Notion with restricted access and kept for up to 12 months after the last interaction, except where retention is legally permitted.

Individuals may request access to, correction or deletion of their data by emailing oes.sumare@gmail.com.
