About the Observatory

Mission and independence

The Sumaré Economic Observatory (OES) is an independent, nonpartisan, free initiative dedicated to improving access to economic and social data on Sumaré, São Paulo, Brazil.

The project brings together indicators from official sources, with a commitment to transparent methods, reliable data and clear communication. Its purpose is to broaden access to knowledge and support evidence-informed public debate.

The OES is not part of a government agency or research institution and does not represent the organizations that produce the statistics it uses.

Mission

Make reliable, verifiable economic and social information about Sumaré freely available in a clear and accessible format.

Vision

Become an independent reference for economic and social information about Sumaré, supporting a better-informed society and evidence-based public discussion.

Values

Scientific rigor · Independence · Transparency · Accessibility · Public interest.

Founder

Aline Araujo Souza is an economist who graduated from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ), with experience in financial and economic analysis, market research, valuation and investments.

She founded the OES to improve access to local economic and social data and contribute to evidence-based public discussion.
