# Website bilingual edition (PT | EN)

- Portuguese remains the default at `site/index.html` and `site/governanca.html`.
- English pages are maintained at `site/en/index.html` and `site/en/governanca.html`.
- Both use the same verified `site/data/indicators.json`. Never duplicate or translate numerical source records.
- English labels for indicator metadata are maintained in `site/assets/js/indicators.js`, keyed by stable indicator ID.
- Original source query identifiers, HTTPS references and evidence checksums remain unchanged for traceability.
- The English edition of the institutional policies must be reviewed when a Portuguese document changes.
- Translation is authored locally. No third-party machine translation, cookies or tracking scripts are loaded.
- Switching languages preserves the section URL fragment; dates and numbers are displayed in the selected locale.
